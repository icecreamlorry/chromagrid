How to play

The idea

Chess is a battle between two armies. White moves first, then players alternate. You win by checkmating the enemy king — attacking it so that it cannot escape capture next move.

New to the game?

Start with Training from the New Game menu — short, guided lessons take you from how each piece moves to checkmate, castling and a few basic tactics.

Making a move

On your turn, tap one of your pieces to select it — its legal moves light up. Tap a highlighted square to move there. When a pawn reaches the far side you choose what it promotes to (usually a queen).

Special moves

Castling tucks your king to safety beside a rook (tap the king, then its two-square destination). En passant lets a pawn capture a pawn that has just rushed two squares past it. Both light up automatically when they're legal.

Per-move timer

The host picks how long each side gets per move — from 1 minute up to 3 days, or Unlimited for no clock at all. If your clock runs out on your move, you lose on time (unless your opponent has too little material to checkmate, which is a draw).

Draws

A game is drawn by stalemate (no legal move but not in check), insufficient material, the 50-move rule, threefold repetition, or by agreement — tap ½ Draw to offer one.

Turn notifications

Tap the 🔔 in the menu to be notified when it becomes your turn while the game is in the background. With Web Push set up, notifications arrive even if the browser is fully closed. On iPhone, first add Chess to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

Offline, leaving & resigning

Chess here is happily asynchronous — an opponent going offline is fine; play resumes when they return (unless their move clock runs out). Leave just exits to your games list. Resign gives up the game for good.
