How to play

The idea

Draughts (checkers) is played on the dark squares of an 8×8 board, 12 pieces a side. You win by capturing or trapping all of your opponent's pieces. White moves first.

Moving & capturing

A man moves one square diagonally forward. To capture, jump an adjacent enemy piece to the empty square just beyond it. Captures are compulsory — if you can take, you must — and if the same piece can keep jumping, it must complete the whole chain.

Kings

A man that reaches the far row is crowned a king (shown with a gold ring). Kings move and capture one square diagonally in any direction, forward or backward.

Per-move timer

The host picks how long each side gets per move — from 1 minute to 3 days, or Unlimited. Run out on your move and you lose on time.

Confirm moves

By default moves are staged — build your move (including a full multi-jump) then press Confirm. Turn it off in the menu to play the moment a move is complete.

Offline, leaving & resigning

An opponent going offline is fine; play resumes when they return. Leave exits to your games list; Resign gives up the game. Offer a ½ Draw any time.
