ANAGRAM HELPER

A nudge for crossword anagrams that doesn't solve them for you — you still get the "aha".

Length — enter how many letters the answer has. Two words? Type 5,4 and the boxes split.

Known letters — type the crossing letters you already have straight into their boxes, crossword-style. They stay put with a solid border; backspace clears one.

Fodder — type every letter of the anagram from the clue. The helper takes out the letters you've locked and deals the rest into the empty boxes.

Shuffle as often as you like until a word leaps out. Spare letters (or a shortfall) are pointed out so you can check your fodder.
