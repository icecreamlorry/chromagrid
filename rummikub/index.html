How to play

The idea

Rummikub uses 106 tiles — numbers 1–13 in four colours (two of each) plus two jokers. You start with 14 on your rack, hidden from everyone else. Be the first to play every tile from your rack to win.

Sets: runs & groups

Everything on the table must be a valid set. A run is 3+ consecutive numbers in one colour (5-6-7 red). A group is 3 or 4 tiles of the same number in different colours. A joker stands in for any tile.

Your opening meld

Your first play must be sets worth 30 or more, made only from your own tiles (a joker counts as the tile it represents). Until then you can't touch the tiles already on the table.

After you've melded

On later turns you may add to and rearrange any sets on the table however you like — as long as you play at least one tile from your rack and every set is still valid when you press Play. Sets highlight green when valid, red when not.

Can't play? Draw.

If you don't want to (or can't) play, press Draw a tile to take one from the pool and end your turn. When the pool is empty you pass instead; a full round of passes ends the game, and the fewest points left in hand wins.

Per-move timer

The host picks how long each player gets per move — 1 minute to 3 days, or Unlimited. Run out and you're out of the game; the others play on.
