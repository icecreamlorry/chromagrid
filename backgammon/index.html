How to play

The idea

Backgammon is a race: move all 15 of your checkers around the board into your home board (bottom-right) and then bear them off. First to bear off all 15 wins.

Rolling & moving

Each turn you roll two dice (shown in the middle). Move a checker for each die — tap a checker, then a highlighted point. Doubles let you play four moves. You must use as many dice as you legally can; press Done when your move is complete (or to pass if you're stuck).

Points, blots & the bar

You can land on any point that isn't held by two or more enemy checkers. Land on a lone enemy checker (a blot) and it's hit — sent to the bar. A player with checkers on the bar must re-enter them (in the opponent's home) before doing anything else.

Bearing off

Once all your checkers are home, bear them off by rolling their exact point number (or higher, if nothing sits further back). Win by a gammon (opponent bore off none) or backgammon (they're also on the bar or in your home) for extra glory.

Per-move timer

The host picks how long each side gets per move — 1 minute to 3 days, or Unlimited. Both players see the same dice (they're rolled fairly from the shared game seed).
