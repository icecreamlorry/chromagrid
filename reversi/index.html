How to play

The idea

Reversi (Othello) is played on an 8×8 board. Dark ● moves first. When the board can take no more moves, whoever has the most discs wins.

Placing & flipping

You may only play where your disc outflanks the opponent: place it so that one or more unbroken lines of their discs sit between your new disc and another of yours — horizontally, vertically or diagonally. Every disc you bracket flips to your colour. A square that flips nothing is not a legal move; the legal ones are marked with a dashed ring.

Skipped turns

If you have no legal move, your turn is skipped automatically and your opponent plays again — you don't need to do anything. When neither side can move, the game ends and the discs are counted.

Which side am I?

Your colour is shown beside your name — Dark ● (filled centre) or Light ○ (open ring). The board is oriented so your discs read the same way every game.

Per-move timer

The host picks how long each side gets per move — from 1 minute to 3 days, or Unlimited. Run out on your move and you lose on time.

Confirm moves

By default moves are staged — tap a square to preview the flips, then press Confirm. Turn it off in the menu to play the instant you tap.

Offline, leaving & resigning

An opponent going offline is fine; play resumes when they return. Leave exits to your games list; Resign gives up the game. Offer a ½ Draw any time.
